// what's inside

Five pieces, each runs where it should

The client in the buyer's browser. Verification on infrastructure you control. Nothing routes through us.

[01]

Payment links + QR

Build monero: URIs and QR client-side. Round-trip tested against the official wallet parser.

[02]

Checkout widget

One self-hosted <xmr-pay> file. No CDN, QR built locally, two skins, en + es.

[03]

Proof verification

Buyer hands you a tx proof; your function re-checks it on-chain against nodes you pick. A lie can't pass.

[04]

Watch mode

Or run your own wallet-rpc and auto-detect. The view key stays on your machine.

[05]

Signed configs + webhooks

Sign the address with a key kept off the server. Fire signed fulfillment webhooks to your own systems.

[06]

Verifiable releases

minisign-signed, reproducible builds. Threat model in the docs. Audit it — it's a payments library.

// this page is the only moving part

This widget is live

The tip jar is a real <xmr-pay> running on this static page — no server behind it. Scan it and you send Monero straight to the author. That's the entire stack.

  • Tips need no backend. Nothing to defraud, nothing to verify.
  • The QR is generated in your browser, right now, from one file.
  • For a store, the same widget points verify-url at your endpoint.
