The whole thing, in five pieces

Each runs where it should — the client in the buyer's browser, verification on infrastructure you control. Nothing routes through us.

Payment links + QR

Build monero: URIs and QR codes client-side. Round-trip tested against the official wallet parser.

Checkout widget

One self-hosted <xmr-pay> file. No CDN, QR generated locally, two skins, English + Spanish.

Proof verification

The buyer hands you a tx proof; your serverless function re-checks it on-chain against nodes you pick. A lie can't pass.

Watch mode

Or run your own wallet-rpc and let payments auto-detect. View key stays on your machine.

Signed configs + webhooks

Sign the address with a key kept off your server; fire signed fulfillment webhooks to your own systems.

Verifiable releases

minisign-signed, reproducible builds. Threat model in the docs. Audit it — it's a payments library.

This widget is live 🍺

The tip jar on the right is a real <xmr-pay> running on this static GitHub Pages site — no server behind it. Scan it, and you're sending Monero straight to the author. That's the whole pitch: the page is the only moving part.

  • Tips need no backend — there's nothing to defraud, so nothing to verify.
  • The QR is generated in your browser, right now, from one file.
  • For a store checkout, the same widget points verify-url at your own endpoint.
