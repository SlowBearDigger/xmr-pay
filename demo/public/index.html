Accept Monero, verified on-chain

The checkout on the left is a real <xmr-pay> widget. Click Try it and the buyer's proof is verified live against stagenet nodes by a function running on this very deploy — no account, no third party. The tip jar on the right is a mainnet address with no backend at all.

Store checkout — stagenet, live verify

A 0.1 XMR order. The "Try it" button fills a real payment proof and submits it to the verify function.

cold start: the verifier loads a Monero WASM wallet on its first call, so the very first verification can take ~20s; after that it's quick.

Tips — mainnet, no backend

Just an address + QR. Nothing to verify, nothing running. Scan to support the project (optional).
