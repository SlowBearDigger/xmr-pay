Tips — mainnet, no backend

      
Drop the address, no verify-url, nothing running. Just a QR + address.

      

        
      

    

    

      
How the verify works

      

        The buyer pays the address, then pastes the wallet's txid + tx proof.
        The widget POSTs them to /api/verify-payment on this deploy, which checks the proof
        against stagenet nodes — confirms the amount, the recipient and the confirmations — and reports
        back. No private keys leave your wallet; the verifier holds none. First call loads a Monero WASM
        wallet (~20s cold start); after that it's quick.
